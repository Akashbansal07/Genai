Without embeddings — keyword search:
  User asks: "How do I cancel my subscription?"
  Search for: documents containing "cancel" AND "subscription"
  Misses: "How to end my plan", "terminate membership",
          "stop recurring payment" — same meaning, different words

With embeddings — semantic search:
  User asks: "How do I cancel my subscription?"
  Embed the question → [0.23, -0.81, ...]
  Embed all documents → [[...], [...], ...]
  Find documents whose vectors are closest to question vector
  Finds: "terminate membership", "stop recurring payment"
         — because they are close in meaning-space
